# Publishing Structure

This file compares what exists in `articles/` with the structure we want to publish on the website.

## Publishing Principle

The research section should feel like a book published as articles.

The user should be able to read linearly from Essay 1 to Essay 12, but also move laterally through concepts:

- Environment
- Light
- Rhythm
- Prediction
- Sensory coherence
- Creative insight and flow
- Collective flow
- State engineering
- Elite performance
- Measurement
- Future performance systems

The public website should also support reader-facing themes. These are broader than concept threads and should feel like useful ways into the research system, not SEO labels:

- Rhythm & Entrainment - Movement synchronising with rhythm.
- Flow State - Challenge, attention and immersion.
- Breath & Nervous System - State regulation.
- Light & Perception - Attention, emotion and atmosphere.
- Music & Emotion - How music shapes experience.

## Desired Public Structure

Public section:

`/research/issue-1/`

Issue:

`Designing Human States`

Subtitle:

`Essays on neuroscience, movement, and environmental design`

Central thesis:

> The next frontier in human performance is not the optimisation of the individual. It is the design of environments that help humans think, move, and connect more effectively.

4. The Predictive Brain Meets the Yoga Studio

## Website Themes

Use the five public themes as reader pathways on `/research/issue-1/` and as landing pages under `/research/themes/`.

| Theme | Public page | What it should collect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rhythm & Entrainment | `/research/themes/rhythm-entrainment` | Essays where timing, synchrony, cadence, group coordination, or auditory-motor entrainment is central. |
| Flow State | `/research/themes/flow-state` | Essays about challenge, attention, immersion, collective flow, measurement, and the conditions that make flow more likely. |
| Breath & Nervous System | `/research/themes/breath-nervous-system` | Essays about regulation, recovery, interoception, autonomic state, and yoga as state training. This theme is currently real but underbuilt until more breath-specific essays are written. |
| Light & Perception | `/research/themes/light-perception` | Essays where light, visual attention, aesthetic perception, atmosphere, circadian biology, or spatial experience shape state. |
| Music & Emotion | `/research/themes/music-emotion` | Essays where music shapes emotion, atmosphere, rhythm, attention, awe, or the felt quality of a class. |

For the website, each theme page should show:

- A short definition.
- Featured issue essays first.
- Companion essays second.
- Related concept threads for deeper navigation.
- A note when a theme is emerging and needs more essays, especially `Breath & Nervous System`.

## Publishing Readiness

Do not publish as final until:

- Every article has original source links.
- Every article ends with Open Questions.
- Every major researcher is introduced through question, method, finding, and why it matters.
- Claims are softened where evidence is preliminary.
- Article 8 is brought up to full research-journal depth.
