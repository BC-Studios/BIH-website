# Cross-References

This map shows how the 10 articles should point to each other.

## Cross-Reference Standard

Each canonical article should include:

- Previous article and next article.
- Builds on: earlier articles needed for context.
- Continues in: later articles that extend the idea.
- Related companion essays.
- Public tags.
- Research base links to source ids, timeline entries, research notes, and cinematic experiment ideas.

Supporting essays should link back into the canonical spine rather than creating a second competing series.
