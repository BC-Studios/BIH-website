# Article Arrangement

This is the working architecture for arranging the Sansara research journal into a coherent public reading experience.

## Core Principle

The main arc should contain only essays that advance the central argument:

> Human performance is not only produced inside the individual. It emerges from the relationship between body, brain, environment, rhythm, attention, skill, regulation, and technology.

Companion essays should deepen, humanise, or apply that argument without being required for the reader to follow the spine.

## Main Arc Criteria

An essay belongs in the main arc when it does at least one of these jobs:

- Introduces a necessary scientific foundation.
- Moves the argument forward in sequence.
- Defines a core Sansara concept.
- Changes how later essays should be understood.
- Carries a major bridge from research into product, teaching, or measurement.

An essay remains a companion essay when it:

- Explores a beautiful adjacent state or theme.
- Applies the main argument to a narrower audience.
- Repeats or earlier-drafts a later synthesis.
- Is useful as atmosphere, evidence, or product philosophy, but not required for the spine.

## Key Promotions

`Beyond Homeostasis` moved into the main arc because state design needs allostasis and predictive regulation before sensory coherence and measurement make full sense.

`Where Do Ideas Come From?` moved into the main arc because it is the strongest current flow-neuroscience essay. It explains expertise plus release, which Article 8 and Article 11 both depend on.

`Engineering Collective Flow` stays in the main arc, but remains `needs_depth`. It should become the group-flow article rather than another general state-design essay.

## Website Arrangement

The website should have:

- `/research/designing-human-states` as the series hub.
- Issue essay pages under `/research/designing-human-states/{slug}`.
- Companion essays under `/research/companion-essays/{slug}`.
- Public tag pages under `/research/tags/{slug}`.
- Research notes under `/research/notes/{slug}`.
- Researcher profiles under `/research/researchers/{slug}`.

The series hub should support two reading modes:

- Linear mode: acts and article order.
- Filter mode: Rhythm & Entrainment, Flow State, Breath & Nervous System, Light & Perception, Music & Emotion.

The research base should stay editorially separate from the polished article pages. It can power source cards, timeline modules, researcher pages, and experiment/cinematic modules without cluttering the essays.
