# Content Overlap Audit

This audit reviews the current readable Markdown drafts for title clarity, repetition, and cross-link coverage.

## Summary

The article system is coherent, but it has two kinds of repetition:

1. Intentional repetition of the central thesis: environment shapes human state.
2. Accidental repetition where earlier synthesis drafts overlap with the canonical spine.

Superseded by the 12-essay structure in `article-arrangement.md`: `Beyond Homeostasis` and `Where Do Ideas Come From?` have since been promoted into the canonical spine. The two earlier manifesto/synthesis pieces should still stay secondary as archive/source material.

## Cross-Link Status

All 17 readable Markdown drafts now include a `Related Reading` section.

Link check:

- All local Markdown links resolve.
- Issue essays link forward/backward through the spine.
- Companion essays link back into the issue essays they enrich.
- Topic references such as rhythm, light, sensory coherence, state engineering, flow, prediction, invisible technology, beauty and awe now have article-level links.

## Highest Overlap Pairs

The strongest overlap pairs are expected:

- `From Exercise Prescription to State Engineering` and `The Next Generation of Human Performance Will Be Environmental`
  - This is acceptable because Article 7 defines the discipline and Article 10 synthesises the full manifesto.
  - Risk: both use broad future-facing environmental-performance language.
  - Fix: keep Article 7 practical/philosophical and Article 10 manifesto/investor-facing.

- `The Next Generation of Human Performance: Designing States, Not Workouts` and `The Next Performance Revolution Won't Happen Inside You`
  - These are earlier synthesis drafts.
  - Risk: if published, they would dilute Article 7 and Article 10.
  - Fix: treat as archive/source material only.

- `The Neuroscience of Environmental Architecture` and `Why Beauty Matters`
  - This overlap is useful.
  - Fix: keep Article 1 about architecture as nervous-system design; keep `Why Beauty Matters` about neuroaesthetics, fluency and soft fascination.

- `The Missing Variable in Modern Wellness` and `Why Beauty Matters`
  - This overlap is useful but needs careful distinction.
  - Fix: beauty is one possible form of coherence, but sensory coherence is not the same as beauty.

## Structural Issues Found

### Article 3 Is Too Thin

`Why Rhythm Is One of the Brain's Oldest Technologies` is about 400 words after links. It currently reads like a concept note.

Recommended expansion:

- Auditory-motor entrainment.
- Neurologic Music Therapy.
- Parkinson's, stroke and gait research.
- Cadence in running, rowing and cycling.
- Group synchrony.
- Rhythm as temporal architecture for class design.

### Article 6 Is Too Thin And Title-Confused

`Engineering Flow: Why the Future of Wellness Is State Design` is about 450 words after links. It overlaps with Article 7 by using the state-design frame.

Recommended expansion:

- Retitle publicly as `Engineering Collective Flow`.
- Make the central question: `Can an entire room enter flow?`
- Add group flow, jazz, sports, dance, choirs, military synchrony, improvisation and shared attention.
- Keep Article 7 as the proper home of `State Engineering`.

### Article 9 Has An Abrupt Ending

`Can We Measure Flow Like We Measure Heart Rate?` currently ends its Research Notes after:

`Contribution: Developed Flow`

Recommended fix:

- Complete Research Notes for Csikszentmihalyi, Kounios/Rosen, Vickers, Swann and measurement methods.
- Add Open Questions.

### Article 5 Needed Heading Cleanup

`The Missing Variable in Modern Wellness` had internal sections extracted as top-level headings. This has been corrected so the article now has one `#` title and section-level `##` headings.

## Editorial Decisions

- Keep the Issue 1 essay spine.
- Keep the strong companion essays: `Why Beauty Matters`, `The Neuroscience of Awe`, `Can Technology Become Invisible?`, and `When Teaching Replaces Practice`.
- Treat the two earlier synthesis essays as archive/source material unless they are substantially rewritten for a new purpose.
- Use `Related Reading` sections as the article-level cross-linking layer.
- Use `article-tags.csv`, `web-link-map.csv` and `research-notes.csv` as the future website data layer.
