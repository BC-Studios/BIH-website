# Research Theme Taxonomy

These themes are intended for the public research website. They should work as reader pathways on the Issue 1 page and as permanent theme landing pages.

Themes are the public-facing entry points. They should stay broad and legible. The deeper sensory relationships should live in [Sensory map](<./sensory-map.md>) and [Sensory map data](<./sensory-map.csv>), where each theme can connect to sensory dimensions, linked systems, and state outcomes.

## Public Themes

| Theme | Slug | Definition | Website use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rhythm & Entrainment | `rhythm-entrainment` | Movement synchronising with rhythm. | Filter page for timing, cadence, synchrony, auditory-motor entrainment, and rhythm as infrastructure. |
| Flow State | `flow-state` | Challenge, attention and immersion. | Filter page for individual flow, collective flow, measurement, attention, and conditions that make flow more likely. |
| Breath & Nervous System | `breath-nervous-system` | State regulation. | Filter page for breath, interoception, autonomic regulation, recovery, yoga, and nervous-system education. This is currently the least developed pillar and should grow as new breath-specific essays arrive. |
| Light & Perception | `light-perception` | Attention, emotion and atmosphere. | Filter page for light, visual attention, perception, circadian biology, spatial atmosphere, and environmental aesthetics. |
| Music & Emotion | `music-emotion` | How music shapes experience. | Filter page for music, emotion, atmosphere, rhythm, awe, attention, and the felt arc of a class. |

## Website Model

On `/research/issue-1/`, themes should behave as broad reader pathways across the essay spine. Each essay can carry multiple themes, but one theme should be marked primary for visual grouping.

Suggested interaction:

- Show all five theme chips near the top of the research index.
- Selecting a theme filters or highlights the relevant essays without leaving the page.
- Each chip should link to a permanent page at `/research/themes/{slug}`.
- Theme pages should show issue essays first, then companion essays.
- Theme pages should keep the book-like essay order inside each group.
- Theme pages should include a sensory fingerprint from `sensory-map.csv`: primary nodes, linked systems, state outcomes, and neighboring themes.

## Editorial Notes

- `Rhythm & Entrainment` and `Music & Emotion` should remain separate. Rhythm is the movement-timing science; music is the emotional and atmospheric layer.
- `Light & Perception` should include more than circadian biology. It should also collect visual attention, mood, aesthetic perception, and atmosphere.
- `Breath & Nervous System` is valid now, but it needs more dedicated essays before it feels as strong as rhythm, light, or flow.
- `Flow State` should include group flow and measurement, not only individual peak experience.
- `Light & Perception` should use circadian rhythm as the primary bridge for light: light -> circadian rhythm -> arousal / recovery -> attention quality -> flow.
- A future `Sensory Coherence` landing page should synthesize all five themes, but it should be a concept page rather than one of the five top-level pathways.
