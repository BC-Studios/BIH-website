Rhythm and entrainment

Can rhythm organise movement and collective timing rather than merely motivate?

Concept Links

Auditory-motor entrainment, rhythmic auditory stimulation, neural resonance, and beat/meter EEG sources linked. Strong evidence supports rhythm as a timing scaffold for movement and rehabilitation; claims about music improving every group class should stay context-specific.

Sources

  • From Sound to Movement: Mapping the Neural Mechanisms of Auditory-Motor Entrainment and Synchronization

    Marija Pranjic; Thenille Braun Janzen; Nikolina Vuksic; Michael Thaut · 2024 · Brain Sciences

    Scoping review of neuroimaging studies on auditory-motor entrainment and synchronization; maps rhythm-to-movement networks including premotor, supplementary motor, basal ganglia, cerebellar, and prefrontal systems.

    PubMedDOI

  • Rhythm Music and the Brain: Scientific Foundations and Clinical Applications

    Michael H. Thaut · 2005 · Routledge

    Foundational book for neurologic music therapy and rhythm-based motor rehabilitation; useful for explaining why rhythm can function as movement infrastructure rather than only entertainment.

    DOI

  • Rhythmic Auditory Stimulation in Gait Training for Parkinson's Disease Patients

    Michael H. Thaut et al. · 1996 · Movement Disorders

    Clinical gait-training study using rhythmic auditory stimulation in Parkinson disease; reports improvements in velocity, stride length, cadence, and gait timing after rhythm-paced training.

    PubMedDOI

  • Neurobiological Foundations of Neurologic Music Therapy: Rhythmic Entrainment and the Motor System

    Michael H. Thaut; Gerald C. McIntosh; Volker Hoemberg · 2015 · Frontiers in Psychology

    Review of rhythmic entrainment as a mechanism for neurologic music therapy, especially how auditory rhythm can couple with motor timing and support movement rehabilitation.

    PubMedDOI

  • Pulse and Meter as Neural Resonance

    Edward W. Large; Joel S. Snyder · 2009 · Annals of the New York Academy of Sciences

    Theoretical neuroscience account proposing that pulse and meter perception can be understood through neural resonance; useful for framing rhythm as predictive temporal structure.

    PubMedDOI

  • Selective Neuronal Entrainment to the Beat and Meter Embedded in a Musical Rhythm

    Sylvie Nozaradan; Isabelle Peretz; Andre Mouraux · 2012 · The Journal of Neuroscience

    EEG study showing enhanced neural responses at perceived beat and meter frequencies, even when those frequencies are not simply dominant in the acoustic signal; useful evidence that the brain constructs rhythmic structure.

    PubMedDOI
