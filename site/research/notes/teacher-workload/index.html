Teacher invisible work

What happens when teaching replaces practice?

Concept Links

Quiet Eye, ecological dynamics, motor learning, cognitive load, and attention-load sources linked. The teacher-workload argument can be framed as hidden attentional, sequencing, timing, and feedback labor; add teacher-specific workload evidence before making occupational-health claims.

Sources

  • The Quiet Eye: Origins Controversies and Future Directions

    Joan N. Vickers · 2016 · Kinesiology Review

    Review of Quiet Eye research tracing how final fixations before movement relate to expertise, decision making, and motor performance across sport and skilled action contexts.

    DOI

  • Ecological Dynamics: A Theoretical Framework for Understanding Sport Performance Physical Education and Physical Activity

    Ludovic Seifert; Keith Davids · 2017 · First Complex Systems Digital Campus World E-Conference 2015

    Theoretical chapter framing skill learning as a person-task-environment system shaped by constraints, affordances, variability, and representative practice.

    DOI

  • Motor Learning

    John W. Krakauer; Adrian M. Hadjiosif; Jing Xu; Aaron L. Wong; Adrian M. Haith · 2019 · Comprehensive Physiology

    Comprehensive review of motor learning mechanisms, including adaptation, skill acquisition, feedback, practice, memory, and transfer.

    PubMedDOI

  • Cognitive Load During Problem Solving: Effects on Learning

    John Sweller · 1988 · Cognitive Science

    Foundational cognitive-load paper arguing that problem-solving formats can consume working-memory resources that would otherwise support learning and schema formation.

    DOI

  • Distracted and confused?: Selective attention under load

    Nilli Lavie · 2005 · Trends in Cognitive Sciences

    Review of load theory showing that perceptual and cognitive load shape how well people filter distractors and maintain selective attention.

    PubMedDOI
