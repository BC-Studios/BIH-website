Elite performance teaching

What can yoga teachers borrow from elite performance science?

Translate elite-performance science into teaching actions around gaze, timing, constraint design, feedback, silence, and recovery; avoid claiming direct yoga outcomes without classroom or studio trials.

Sources

  • The Quiet Eye: Origins Controversies and Future Directions

    Joan N. Vickers · 2016 · Kinesiology Review

    Review of Quiet Eye research tracing how final fixations before movement relate to expertise, decision making, and motor performance across sport and skilled action contexts.

    DOI

  • Motor Learning

    John W. Krakauer; Adrian M. Hadjiosif; Jing Xu; Aaron L. Wong; Adrian M. Haith · 2019 · Comprehensive Physiology

    Comprehensive review of motor learning mechanisms, including adaptation, skill acquisition, feedback, practice, memory, and transfer.

    PubMedDOI

  • Ecological Dynamics: A Theoretical Framework for Understanding Sport Performance Physical Education and Physical Activity

    Ludovic Seifert; Keith Davids · 2017 · First Complex Systems Digital Campus World E-Conference 2015

    Theoretical chapter framing skill learning as a person-task-environment system shaped by constraints, affordances, variability, and representative practice.

    DOI

  • Creative flow as optimized processing: Evidence from brain oscillations during jazz improvisations by expert and non-expert musicians

    David Rosen; Yongtaek Oh; Christine Chesebrough; Fengqing Zoe Zhang; John Kounios · 2024 · Neuropsychologia

    EEG study of jazz guitar improvisation linking higher flow and expertise with reduced frontal/executive activity and greater posterior/central activity, consistent with optimized processing in skilled creative performance.

    PubMedDOI

  • Predictions not commands: active inference in the motor system

    Rick A. Adams; Stewart Shipp; Karl J. Friston · 2013 · Brain Structure and Function

    Review proposing that motor control can be understood through active inference: actions fulfil proprioceptive predictions rather than merely executing top-down commands.

    PubMedDOI
