Flow measurement

Can quality of experience be estimated without relying only on self-report?

Concept Links

Flow theory, jazz-improvisation EEG, Quiet Eye, attention-load, and yoga self-regulation sources linked. Current evidence supports triangulating self-report with task context, gaze/attention, physiology, and behavior; any Sansara flow score should be presented as exploratory until validated.

Sources

  • The flow experience and its significance for human psychology

    Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi · 1988 · Optimal Experience

    Foundational flow chapter describing flow as deep task absorption shaped by clear goals, feedback, concentration, intrinsic reward, and the relation between challenge and skill.

    DOI

  • Creative flow as optimized processing: Evidence from brain oscillations during jazz improvisations by expert and non-expert musicians

    David Rosen; Yongtaek Oh; Christine Chesebrough; Fengqing Zoe Zhang; John Kounios · 2024 · Neuropsychologia

    EEG study of jazz guitar improvisation linking higher flow and expertise with reduced frontal/executive activity and greater posterior/central activity, consistent with optimized processing in skilled creative performance.

    PubMedDOI

  • The Quiet Eye: Origins Controversies and Future Directions

    Joan N. Vickers · 2016 · Kinesiology Review

    Review of Quiet Eye research tracing how final fixations before movement relate to expertise, decision making, and motor performance across sport and skilled action contexts.

    DOI

  • Distracted and confused?: Selective attention under load

    Nilli Lavie · 2005 · Trends in Cognitive Sciences

    Review of load theory showing that perceptual and cognitive load shape how well people filter distractors and maintain selective attention.

    PubMedDOI

  • Potential self-regulatory mechanisms of yoga for psychological health

    Tim Gard; Jessica J. Noggle; Crystal L. Park; David R. Vago; Angela Wilson · 2014 · Frontiers in Human Neuroscience

    Review proposing self-regulatory pathways through which yoga may affect psychological health, including attention control, emotion regulation, body awareness, and stress physiology.

    PubMedDOI
