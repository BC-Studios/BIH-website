Discovery

University research story · 2003

In 2003, university cognitive psychology labs began catching the Aha moment before it fully arrived in consciousness. Edward Bowden and Mark Beeman studied insight problems and showed that a solution could be partially active before a person could explain how they knew.

Result: The result made creative insight less mystical without making it mechanical. Ideas can emerge from preparation, unconscious activation and reduced interference. For Sansara, this supports the link between environment, attention, flow and creative release.

Sources

  • The cognitive neuroscience of insight

    John Kounios; Mark Beeman · 2014 · Annual Review of Psychology

    Review synthesizing behavioral and neural work on insight problem solving, including preparation, unconscious processing, neural precursors, and Aha experience.

    PubMedDOI

  • Aha! Insight experience correlates with solution activation in the right hemisphere

    Edward M. Bowden; Mark Jung-Beeman · 2003 · Psychonomic Bulletin & Review

    Experimental insight paper showing that reported Aha experiences correlate with solution-related activation patterns, supporting the idea that solutions can be partly activated before conscious report.

    PubMedDOI

  • Creative flow as optimized processing: Evidence from brain oscillations during jazz improvisations by expert and non-expert musicians

    David Rosen; Yongtaek Oh; Christine Chesebrough; Fengqing Zoe Zhang; John Kounios · 2024 · Neuropsychologia

    EEG study of jazz guitar improvisation linking higher flow and expertise with reduced frontal/executive activity and greater posterior/central activity, consistent with optimized processing in skilled creative performance.

    PubMedDOI

  • Cognitive Load During Problem Solving: Effects on Learning

    John Sweller · 1988 · Cognitive Science

    Foundational cognitive-load paper arguing that problem-solving formats can consume working-memory resources that would otherwise support learning and schema formation.
