Research Notes 30 linked research notes
linked Window-view recovery effect Ulrich 1984 found that postsurgical patients with tree views recovered faster and used fewer strong analgesics than patients facing a brick wall. Environment / Light / Space / Rhythm linked Nonvisual light pathway ipRGC and melanopsin studies show that light signals circadian timing and physiology beyond image-forming vision. Environment / Light / Space / Sequence linked Rhythmic auditory stimulation for gait Thaut and related rhythm studies show that external pulse can scaffold motor timing in neurological movement tasks. Environment / Light / Space / Rhythm linked Predictive processing and active inference Helmholtz Friston Clark Seth and Adams support the theory that perception and action are shaped by prediction rather than passive input. Environment / Light / Space / Sequence linked Multisensory coherence under load Multisensory integration and attention-load research shows that coherent timing and sensory alignment reduce avoidable processing conflict. Environment / Light / Space / Rhythm linked Flow as skilled absorption Csikszentmihalyi and later EEG work support flow as task absorption shaped by skill challenge feedback expertise and reduced interference. Environment / Light / Space / Rhythm linked Insight before conscious report Insight studies show that solutions can become partially active before conscious awareness and are shaped by preparation and reduced interference. Environment / Light / Space / Rhythm linked Affordances and predictive state design Gibson Friston Sterling and distributed cognition research support designing environments as action-regulation systems rather than backdrops. Environment / Light / Space / Rhythm linked Quiet Eye and ecological skill learning Quiet Eye motor-learning and ecological-dynamics research show that expertise depends on attention timing feedback constraints and adaptable practice. Environment / Light / Space / Rhythm linked Triangulating flow measurement Flow measurement research supports combining self-report task context gaze physiology behavior and neural signals rather than relying on one score. Environment / Light / Space / Rhythm linked Internal environment and allostasis Bernard Sterling Seth and yoga self-regulation research support regulation as embodied prediction across physiology attention and breath. Environment / Light / Space / Sequence linked Awe as vastness plus accommodation Keltner Haidt Piff and neuroaesthetics sources distinguish awe beauty and restoration as related but different shifts in attention meaning and self-focus. Environment / Light / Space / Rhythm linked Distributed cognition and invisible interfaces Hutchins Hollan Kirsh Gibson and load-theory sources show that tools can carry cognition when interfaces reduce representational friction. Environment / Light / Space / Rhythm linked Human-centered design and cognitive usability Don Norman's design work frames intuitive technology as the reduction of unnecessary cognitive effort through affordances mappings feedback and clear evaluation. Environment / Light / Space / Attention linked Interface conventions as invisible intention Apple interface design lineage frames technology as most successful when conventions become extensions of user intention rather than obstacles. Environment / Light / Space / Attention linked Neural efficiency under expertise Neural efficiency research suggests experts can perform some complex tasks with less unnecessary neural recruitment but only under specific expertise and task-demand conditions. Environment / Light / Space / Rhythm linked Cognitive load of teaching Cognitive-load Quiet Eye ecological dynamics and motor-learning research frame teaching as hidden attention sequencing timing and feedback labour. Environment / Light / Space / Rhythm linked Beat perception and motor preparation Beat perception research shows that listening to rhythm can recruit motor planning regions even without overt movement. Environment / Light / Space / Rhythm linked Pulse and meter as neural resonance Large and Snyder frame rhythm perception as dynamic neural resonance between external pulse and internal timing systems. Environment / Light / Space / Rhythm linked Music effects in exercise and sport Music meta-analysis suggests effects on affect perceived exertion performance and physiological efficiency but mechanisms depend on context preference synchrony and task. Environment / Light / Space / Rhythm linked Homeostasis and internal stability Walter Cannon's homeostasis model frames physiology as feedback regulation that protects internal stability under changing external conditions. Environment / Light / Space / Sequence linked Allostasis as stability through change Sterling and Eyer's allostasis framework reframes regulation as anticipatory adaptation to predicted demand rather than return to one fixed set point. Environment / Light / Space / Rhythm linked Allostatic load and stress cost McEwen's allostatic-load work explains how adaptive stress mediators can become costly when repeatedly or chronically activated. Environment / Light / Space / Sequence linked Interoception and embodied prediction Craig Critchley Garfinkel Barrett and Seth support the view that bodily feeling is actively interpreted rather than passively read out. Environment / Light / Space / Sequence linked Unconscious inference in perception Helmholtz's unconscious-inference lineage frames perception as active interpretation from incomplete sensory evidence. Environment / Light / Space / Sequence linked Affordances and perception-action coupling Gibson's ecological psychology shows that perception is oriented toward possible action within environments. Environment / Light / Space / Rhythm linked Awe small self and prosocial behaviour Awe studies link vastness and accommodation with reduced self-focus and increased prosocial tendencies while preserving individual variability. Environment / Light / Space / Rhythm linked Restoration and awe in environments Environmental psychology and awe research separate gentle attention restoration from expansive shifts in meaning and self-focus. Environment / Light / Space / Rhythm linked Visual neuroaesthetics and beauty Zeki's neuroaesthetics work treats beauty as a legitimate subject of visual neuroscience and aesthetic perception. Environment / Light / Space / Rhythm linked Aesthetic cognition and decision making Chatterjee's aesthetic-brain synthesis links beauty to interacting systems for perception attention emotion memory and meaning.
