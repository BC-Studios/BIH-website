# Production Workflow

Use this workflow for each research essay.

## 1. Intake

Create a new file in `content/research/` from `_essay-template.md`.

Fill in:

- Working title
- Thesis
- Raw notes
- Papers and links
- Researchers
- Unknowns
- Movement-design implications

## 2. Source Library

Every source mentioned in an essay should also appear in `content/library/references.csv`.

Use a stable source id such as:

- `ulrich-1984-view-window`
- `friston-2010-free-energy`
- `kaplan-1995-restorative-environments`

## 3. Chronology

Before writing the main essay, build the chronological story:

- What was the original question?
- Which experiment changed the field?
- Which theory reframed the question?
- What is the current frontier?

## 4. Essay Draft

Write the essay using the eight-part architecture in `_essay-template.md`. Keep the research notes section factual and link-rich.

## 5. Editorial Review

Check:

- Does the opening question create intellectual tension?
- Does the opening begin with a paradox rather than a fact?
- Is the industry misconception specific?
- Are landmark researchers introduced through question, method, finding, and why it matters?
- Does each major claim separate what we know from what we do not know?
- Are uncertainties treated honestly?
- Are movement implications concrete?
- Are Sansara concepts used as language rather than marketing?
- Does the article avoid filler, wellness cliches, AI tone, and unsupported claims?
- Does the article end with Open Questions?
- Are all original sources linked?

## 6. Web Preparation

Before handoff to the website:

- Confirm title, subtitle, and one-sentence thesis.
- Confirm reading time and difficulty.
- Confirm researcher names and affiliations.
- Confirm source links, DOI links, and book links.
- Add hero image direction or illustration brief.
